The Events

Thirteen Ways to Win

Every event scores one point for the boat that wins it. Style is unscored but not unnoticed.

Water

Duckie Relay

Swimming relay, duckie in hand

A relay swim with one non-negotiable rule: the rubber duck never touches water. Drop it, redo the lap.

Ropes

Buoy Knotting Sprint

Fastest to knot all the buoys

A line of buoys, one clock. First crew to knot every single one wins — sloppy knots don't count.

Water

Ring Dive

Jump through rings

Timed obstacle jumps through a line of floating rings. Precision beats speed.

Stage

Guess the Song

Noran sings

One microphone, one merciless judge, endless off-key nostalgia. Name the tune before the chorus.

Social

The Paloma Sprint

Drink cocktail challenge — fastest one down

Fastest crew member to finish a Paloma, properly. Pun fully intended, dignity optional.

Water

Captain Overboard

Drop your captains, fastest team to collect

Captains go in first. Crew retrieves them fastest wins. Loyalty, tested in real time.

Ropes

The Knot Challenge

Tie it right, tie it fast

Bowline, clove hitch, figure-eight — the classics, against the clock and each other.

Water

Water Ballet Lift

Lift a teammate

Synchronized swimming's finest move, performed by amateurs in open water. Style points included.

Water

SUP Circuit

SUP race around the boat

Paddle-board sprint, full loop around the mothership. Fall in, start over.

Water

SUP Buoy Haul

Full team, one board, five minutes

The entire crew on one SUP. Five minutes to untie and haul in as many buoys as possible — without anyone falling in.

Stage

Make a Song

Instruments — whatever's on board

Whatever's on the boat is an instrument now. Compose, perform, own it.

Stage

Choreography Face-off

Dancing competition

Full routine, full commitment. Judged on nerve as much as rhythm.

Stage

Sailing Stories

Told as theatre

A real (or generously embellished) sailing story, staged as a five-minute performance.

Five Days

The Arc

Skill first, then nerve, then the stage. By day five, everyone's earned their story.
